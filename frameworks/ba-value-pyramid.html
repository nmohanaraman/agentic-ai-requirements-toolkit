F-06 · AI-Augmented BA Operating Toolkit

BA Value Pyramid

A map of where your time creates value — and where the automation zone begins.

01 · What problem it solves

AI is not coming for the analyst's entire role; it is coming for the bottom of the value stack. Before deciding what to automate, you need a map of where your current work sits. The pyramid organizes BA work into five levels by the judgment, domain context, stakeholder influence, and accountability each task requires — from highly automatable at the base to irreplaceable at the apex.

02 · How it works

LevelWorking definition
5 — Strategic judgmentCross-functional trade-offs, risk framing, decisions with enterprise consequences
4 — Domain synthesisDeep domain analysis, negotiation, complex requirements, architecture and risk reasoning
3 — Applied analysisStructured analysis requiring review, interpretation, or context
2 — Repeatable knowledge workSummaries, standard documentation, routine validation, templated analysis
1 — Administrative / mechanicalData entry, formatting, status compilation, repetitive updates

How to use it: audit your last two sprints against real evidence — time logs, backlog history, meeting records — and categorize every task. If a large share of your time sits at Levels 1–2, that is the signal to shift workload upward and skill investment with it. End the audit with a quantified mix, a target mix by next review, and a shift plan naming which higher-value work receives each hour freed. A quarterly review turns the audit into an operating rhythm.

03 · Worked example

An analyst on a logistics company's order-management platform audits two sprints (64 logged hours): 24h at Levels 1–2 (status compilation, ticket triage notes, formatting release summaries), 28h at Level 3, 12h at Levels 4–5 (carrier-integration data contract, peak-season risk assessment). Shift plan: automate status compilation from the tracker (Level 1, ~5h/sprint) and AI-draft release summaries with human review (Level 2, ~4h/sprint); the freed hours are explicitly reassigned to the carrier contract negotiation. Target: Levels 1–2 under a third of logged time by next quarter, verified against the same time logs.

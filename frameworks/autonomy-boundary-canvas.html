F-02 · Agentic AI Requirements Toolkit

Autonomy Boundary Canvas

Turns “the agent can help with X” into explicit, stakeholder-approved authority.

01 · What problem it solves

The single highest-value artifact an analyst can produce for an agentic system is an explicit statement of the agent's authority. Without it, the sponsor imagines an assistant, engineering builds an operator, and compliance prices an intern — three different agents, discovered in production. The Canvas structures the authority conversation into three columns and forces the arguments that matter: thresholds, named approvers, and prohibitions that encode the institution's risk appetite.

02 · How it works

MAY ACT — independentlyMUST ASK — approval requiredMUST NEVER — prohibited
Action + object + condition + threshold. Entries without thresholds are not boundaries.Action + trigger + a named approver and channel — or the entry is theater.Prohibited action + rationale. These deserve the most scrutiny.

Each rule is registered with an ID, boundary class, condition, approver, required evidence, and a review trigger. Graduation rule: a MUST ASK action moves to MAY ACT only after predefined evidence supports the change and an authorized stakeholder approves it — the canvas doubles as the agent's graduation plan, and autonomy never expands by drift. Governance reviews of the canvas map onto the human-oversight obligations that Article 14 of the EU AI Act imposes on high-risk systems.

03 · Worked example

Retail returns agent, agreed in a 90-minute workshop with the returns manager, a finance controller, and one engineer:

IDRuleClassCondition / approver
AB-001Issue refund to original payment methodMAYItem scanned received; refund ≤ $75; within 30-day window
AB-002Issue refund $75–$400ASKQueue to returns supervisor; 4-business-hour SLA; timeout = hold
AB-003Waive restocking feeASKOnly when carrier damage evidence attached; supervisor approves
AB-004Refund above $400, or any gift-card conversionNEVERRoute to human agent; fraud exposure
AB-005Send any communication outside the returns threadNEVERNo external email, SMS, or social contact

AB-002 carries a graduation clause: after 500 supervised approvals with an override rate below an agreed threshold, stakeholders may vote to raise the MAY ACT ceiling — by documented decision, recorded in change control.

F-09 · AI-Augmented BA Operating Toolkit

5-Day AI Sprint

The intervention: one task, real data, documented failures, and a decision — in five days.

01 · What problem it solves

The first three frameworks are diagnostic; this one acts. Most AI experimentation fails by starting with “use AI” instead of a precise work problem with a baseline — and by never documenting what broke. The sprint is a repeatable protocol: run it monthly on a different use case and you build systematic integration competence with an evidence trail. A pilot that produces no documented decision is just a demo.

02 · How it works

DayStepOutput
1MAP the current human processEvery step, handoff, decision point, and pain — documented, not optimized
2IDENTIFY candidates via the Task Triage MatrixAUGMENT / AUTOMATE steps with the exact AI action: draft, classify, validate, summarize
3SPEC inputs, outputs, constraintsWhat AI receives and must produce; must-never rules; escalation rule; logging
4PILOT on real data — the most important dayA test log and failure-pattern summary: what it got wrong, how often, and why
5DOCUMENT and decideBefore/after scorecard; go / revise / stop; triage-matrix update; next experiment

The charter set on day 0 fixes the baseline, the approved data boundary, stop conditions, and the accountable human owner — so the pilot cannot drift into production by enthusiasm.

03 · Worked example

Use case: delivery-exception triage, where an analyst reviews a daily queue of flagged shipments by hand. Day 1 maps the nine-step manual flow. Day 2 marks classification as AUGMENT and root-cause suggestion as PROTECT. Day 3 specifies: input is the exception record and carrier scan history; output is a category and confidence; must never contact a customer or change shipment state; low confidence escalates to the analyst. Day 4 runs two weeks of real exception data and finds a systematic failure: the model misclassifies exceptions on multi-leg international shipments — a domain-specific pattern no generic benchmark would surface. Day 5's decision: adopt with a governance rule routing every multi-leg international flag to human review regardless of confidence, and log that rule in the triage matrix. Skip day 4, and the team meets that failure mode in production instead.
